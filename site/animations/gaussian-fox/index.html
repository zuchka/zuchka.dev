Gaussian splatting · 0:36

What makes this fox look solid?

Soft blobs of color overlap to draw the fox. At the eyelid, their colors blend into a smooth edge.

Transcript and sources

Visual transcript

0:00–0:05 A seated porcelain fox comes into view. Follow the black rim of its eye as the camera moves in.

0:05–0:11 The camera passes through the eyelid and isolates one splat. A splat is a soft blob of color with a position and shape in 3D.

0:11–0:16 A marker traces the splat from its center toward its fading edge. Its color contributes most at the center.

0:16–0:22 Back at the eye, only one in eight splats is visible. What will fill the gaps? The camera holds still for two seconds.

0:22–0:29 The remaining splats return. From the same angle, watch their colors overlap and blend into a smooth eyelid.

0:29–0:36 The camera pulls back to the whole fox. The same 76,587 splats draw it from each angle. This is called Gaussian splatting.

Sources

The fox was sculpted in Blender. Brush fitted its 76,587 splats from 48 rendered views. The close-up follows a single fitted splat on the near eyelid. Brush

The rendering method is described by Kerbl, Kopanas, Leimkühler, and Drettakis in 3D Gaussian Splatting for Real-Time Radiance Field Rendering (2023). Paper and project

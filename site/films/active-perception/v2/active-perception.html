A robot needs to pick up a mug by its handle. Each dot represents a point its camera sees. Zooming enlarges the same front image, where the mug hides the handle. The robot moves its camera sideways to see the opening. The gripper fits one finger through the handle, closes the other on the outside and lifts the mug. Roboticists call this active perception. The robot chooses what to sense next based on the information it needs.

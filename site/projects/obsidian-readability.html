obsidian-readability

A tiny Obsidian plugin that scores your prose.

Calculates the Flesch Reading Ease score for the active document and surfaces it as a status-bar number. Helps you notice when a paragraph is harder to read than it needs to be.

What it does

The Flesch Reading Ease formula maps prose to a 0–100 score: 60–70 is "plain English," 30–50 is "college-level," anything below 30 is hard going. The plugin computes the score for whatever you're editing and shows it in Obsidian's status bar. As you write, the number moves. 

FRE = 206.835 − 1.015 × (words / sentences) − 84.6 × (syllables / words)

Two knobs: shorter sentences, simpler words. The score is a stand-in for both. It's not a writing instructor, but it's a useful nudge.

Install

Available in the Obsidian community plugins store. Inside Obsidian:

  • Settings → Community plugins → Browse
  • Search "Readability" and install
  • Enable — the FRE score appears in the status bar

Why

I wanted live feedback on prose density without context-switching to a separate tool. The score isn't gospel — technical writing legitimately scores low — but it catches the times you accidentally pile clauses on top of clauses without noticing.
